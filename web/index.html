FC Solver: SBC solver and SBC builder for EA FC 27, from your own club

FC Solver is an automatic SBC solver: pick a Squad Building Challenge and press Solve. It builds the squad from the players you already own, including your SBC storage, and spends your least valuable cards first. Every requirement, rating and chemistry is checked the way the game does it. You rebuild the squad in the EA FC web app yourself: FC Solver never buys, sells or submits anything.
