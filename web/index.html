FC Solver: the cheapest EA FC 27 SBC squad from your own club

Pick a Squad Building Challenge and press Solve. FC Solver builds it from the players you already own, including your SBC storage, and spends your least valuable cards first. Every requirement, rating and chemistry is checked the way the game does it. You rebuild the squad in the EA FC web app yourself: FC Solver never buys, sells or submits anything.
