# Product

## Register

product

## Users
FC27 Ultimate Team players (the owner and friends), each with their own EA account. They sit at a desk in the evening with the EA FC web app open in one tab and this builder in another, or check it on a phone between matches. Job: "which squad from MY club completes this SBC while burning the least valuable cards?", then build it by hand in the web app.

## Product Purpose
Reads the player's club and the live SBC list (never submits anything to EA) and shows the cheapest valid squad on a pitch that looks like the one in the web app, so copying it over is effortless. Success: open an SBC, press Solve, get a correct squad in seconds, rebuild it in the web app without second-guessing.

## Brand Personality
Native, fast, trustworthy. It should feel like a missing tab of the EA web app, not a third-party tool: same dark teal pitch, same card art, same position pills. Confidence comes from exactness (requirement ticks, chemistry dots, rating stars that match the game).

## Anti-references
- Generic AI dashboards: purple gradients, glass cards, hero-metric tiles, emoji icons.
- Futbin / FUT.GG ad-heavy, cluttered tables.
- Anything that makes the pitch smaller than the chrome around it.

## Design Principles
1. The pitch is the product; everything else is secondary chrome.
2. Mirror the game's vocabulary and visuals exactly (card art, positions, chemistry 0 to 3, stars).
3. Show certainty: every requirement is visibly met or not.
4. Never waste EA requests: data is cached, sync is explicit.

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast on text and controls, keyboard reachable controls, prefers-reduced-motion respected, requirement state never conveyed by color alone (tick / cross glyphs).
